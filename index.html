What we do

Where ideas stall, why, and what might help.

Good ideas in education and workforce often stall between research and the people they were meant to serve. EvaNexus convenes the people involved at each step, diagnoses where and why an idea loses momentum, and co-designs practical responses with them. We turn what cross-sector leaders tell us into shared knowledge the whole field can use: where ideas stall, why, and what might help.

Mission

EvaNexus brings together the people who build, fund, implement, and experience education and workforce systems to understand where and why promising ideas stall, and to shape the solutions and frameworks that help those systems work better together, and translate promising ideas into meaningful outcomes.

Vision

A world where the value of research and innovation is measured not only by what is developed, but by what reaches people, creating opportunities for them to thrive, build lasting economic mobility, and shape their own futures.

How we do it

Why this work matters

Billions of dollars go into education and workforce ideas, yet many never reach the learners and workers they serve. The idea itself is rarely the problem. A research study finds something that works, but schools never hear about it, or can’t use it as designed. A program launches, but the people it was built for never enroll.

Each step depends on the relationships, practices, and systems that connect one stage to the next, and no one is responsible for them. We call these the handoffs. EvaNexus works there.
